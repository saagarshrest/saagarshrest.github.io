Privacy Policy

Coin Commando and Kathmandu Shadows · Effective 8 November 2026

In short: these games collect, sell and share nothing. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

About this policy

This policy covers the Android apps Coin Commando (package np.com.saagarshrest.coincommando) and Kathmandu Shadows (package np.com.saagarshrest.kathmandushadows), made by Saagar Shrestha.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, each game keeps a small save inside the app on your device:

  • Levels unlocked and the furthest endless level reached
  • Scores, coins, ranks and collected shards
  • Settings such as sound and screen shake

This save never leaves your device through the game.

What we collect

Nothing. The games have:

  • no account or sign-in
  • no advertisements
  • no in-app purchases
  • no analytics, tracking or crash reporting
  • no internet permission; they play fully offline

The only time anything opens online is if you tap a link to the developer's website; that opens in your own web browser.

Permissions

The games request no special permissions. They do not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Backups

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, your phone may include the game save in that backup. That is handled by your device and your account, not by the developer.

Deleting your data

Starting a new game from the title screen resets progress. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game deletes the save permanently.

Children

The games collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
